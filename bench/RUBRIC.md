# Human review rubric

Score each output 1–5 on each criterion. Use the whole scale: 3 means "fine, unremarkable". Judge what's on the page, not what you think the model intended.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Specific to this idea** | Could be pasted onto any startup | Mostly about this idea, some filler | Every line only makes sense for this pitch |
| **Realistic reasoning** | Invented statistics, impossible economics, made-up competitors | Broadly sensible, a few loose claims | Sound reasoning; numbers framed as assumptions or derived from the pitch |
| **Funny** | Flat, or just insulting | A smile here and there | Lines you'd screenshot and share |
| **Distinct personas** | Four copies of one voice | Recognisable roles, similar tone | Unmistakably STERLING, KERNEL, HYPE and WALLET, in lane and in voice |
| **Real debate** | Four monologues taking turns | Some back-and-forth | They answer each other, concede, push back and land on the crux |
| **Useful advice** | Nothing actionable | Generic advice ("validate your market") | You know exactly what to change and why |
| **FIX MY IDEA quality** | Cosmetic rewording | Some real changes, some fluff | A materially stronger idea that answers the panel's objections, with concrete next steps |

Notes are optional, but write one whenever you score 1 or 5: those are the examples that improve the prompts.
