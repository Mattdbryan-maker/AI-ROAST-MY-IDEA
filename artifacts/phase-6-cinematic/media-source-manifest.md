# Media source manifest

This trailer mixes **promotional animation** with **real app footage replaying stored real model output**. Nothing is a fresh live session. No paid API request, voice service, stock library or licensed music was used.

## Classes

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Real model output (historical)** | Text generated by Claude Haiku 5.5 during the 2026-10-08 benchmark, prompt version `2026-10-08.4`, idea `bad-nft-sourdough`. File: `bench/results/2026-10-08T19-08-52-final-10/results.json`. Replayed, not regenerated. |
| **Real app UI** | Footage of the actual running app (`next start`, `AI_PROVIDER=demo`) in headless Chromium, with the stored output fed through the app's own streaming endpoints by request interception. Real components, animation and pacing. |
| **Promotional animation** | The courtroom, the four judges, props and effects. Drawn in code for this trailer. **Not an app feature.** |
| **Editorial** | Titles, jokes, captions and short-hands written for the trailer. |

## Staged / fictional elements (promotional animation only)

Everything the judges *do* and every prop: the courtroom, judges' faces and acting, Sterling's "£0.00" calculator and rejection stamp, Kernel's overheating, steam, sparks, the blue screen reading ":( FATAL: BLOCKCHAIN / rebooting to insult you...", and ERR face, Hype's shades and notification bubbles ("99+"), Wallet's empty wallet with a "1p" coin and his throwing a receipt and leaving, the red flashes, shockwaves and camera shake. **None of this exists in the app**, and the real app does not animate the judges.

**Two things to know before publishing:**
1. The app shows its "live AI" badge because the replayed stream announces `mode: "ai"`. The plate states the truth.
2. The stored FIX pitch (prompt `.4`) begins "I bake sourdough at home and I kept losing track of where my starters came from…", an **invented founder backstory** that the later safeguards (Phase 4 / 5) would strip or reject. It is visible, partly covered by the disclosure plate, on the FIX-result clip (about 1 s). If you re-capture with a live run on the current prompts, this disappears.

## Audio

All audio is **synthesised from code** (`scripts/trailer/audio.mjs`): oscillators, filtered noise, envelopes. No samples, no music library, no text-to-speech, no voice cloning, no recorded speech. The characters' "voices" are pitched babble (Sterling low and smooth, Kernel quantised bit-blips, Hype gliding sparkle, Wallet small squeaks). Effects are sine/noise/bell tones; the "sad trombone" is a generic descending sawtooth idiom, not a recording.
